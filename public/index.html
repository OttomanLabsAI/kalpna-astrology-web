About Kalpna

Kalpna is a London-based astrologer here to empower you with astrology and unlock the magic of your own potential.

There is a whole universe inside a person; your birth chart is a compass to your own intuition. It affirms the subtle nudges you have always felt and the bigger dreams you hold, even the ones you do not fully believe are achievable yet. 

Kalpna is so passionate about people knowing their birth charts because self-knowledge changes the way you live. When you understand all aspects of yourself, your strengths, your sensitivities, and your contradictions, you stop trying to mould yourself into someone else’s expectations. You begin to live originally.

She is particularly drawn to the Rising sign, which she sees as the thread running through your life. It reveals your patterns and the direction you are moving into in this lifetime. While most people are taught to focus on their Sun sign, many feel a deeper resonance with their Rising sign because it reflects who they are becoming.

Through her work, Kalpna helps you understand yourself with clarity and warmth, so you can move forward with confidence and trust in yourself.
