About Kalpna

A whole universe, inside you.

Kalpna is a London-based astrologer here to empower you with astrology and unlock the magic of your own potential.

Your birth chart is a compass to your own intuition. It affirms the subtle nudges you have always felt and the bigger dreams you hold — even the ones you do not fully believe are achievable yet.

Kalpna is passionate about people knowing their birth charts because self-knowledge changes the way you live. When you understand every aspect of yourself — your strengths, your sensitivities, your contradictions — you stop trying to mould yourself into someone else’s expectations.

You begin to live originally.
